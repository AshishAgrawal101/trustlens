Reported percentages

Enter a number from 0 to 100 without a percent sign. Use a decimal point if needed, without commas or exponents; up to 64 characters. Underlying counts and totals are not required and are not inferred. Reported percentages may already be rounded.

Context to check before drawing a conclusion

    What this comparison cannot establish

    These calculations describe the supplied values. They do not verify the numbers or their sources, measure statistical significance, establish causality or determine whether a claim is true.

    The groups may differ in size, definitions, selection, time period or circumstances. A difference in rates does not explain why it occurred. Rounded display values can hide small differences.

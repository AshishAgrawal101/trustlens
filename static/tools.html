01 / COMMUNICATION

What is the request asking you to do?

Message checks look for rule-based warning signs in submitted wording and literal addresses. Screenshot text must be read, corrected and confirmed before checking.

Message →Screenshot →Link →Email →Call transcript →QR image →

Each choice opens its input without running a check. You can also select Link, Email, Call and QR in the checker’s More tools. Link examines an address without visiting it; Email checks supplied wording and headers, leaving attachments unscanned; Call checks a transcript, not a voice; QR reads text without opening its destination.

No warning does not establish safety. Sender identity, hidden destinations and the surrounding story remain unverified.

02 / CONTEXT

What would help you assess the claim?

Claim & context →

Organize a specific claim, source accounts and your own evidence labels. Compare supplied publication dates with a claimed event date, then plan what to check independently.

Media & context →

Read file dimensions and selected editable image metadata. Keep the caption, event, date and location unresolved.

Numbers & context →

Compare counts and totals or two reported percentages. Separate percentage points and relative change; percentages alone do not supply sample sizes.

Quote & context →

Compare supplied text versions, or find exact wording in a longer pasted passage. Inspect surrounding context; a wording match does not authenticate a source.

Ads & endorsements →

Use fixed questions to examine a recommendation, its incentives and the evidence behind a product claim. The guide does not detect fake reviews.

These tools do not fetch sources, establish independent evidence or determine whether a claim is true. Your source labels and supplied material remain unverified.

03 / IMAGE ORIGIN

What origin signals are available?

In the checker’s More tools, choose Image origin to inspect available visual-model and Content Credentials signals. The visual model has not met release requirements and can be unavailable or inconclusive.

Open Image origin →

Signed provenance and visual-model output are separate evidence channels. Missing credentials do not identify AI generation. Neither channel verifies a caption, depicted event, seller or payment request.

Keep the limits beside the result.

Text context worksheets run locally in your browser on their own pages. File inspection, screenshot reading, QR decoding and image-origin checks require the desktop launcher and their installed components. This directory does not run checks or send your input anywhere.

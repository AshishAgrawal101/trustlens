## Fifteen-minute voluntary session

Ask permission to take anonymous notes. Participation is optional; participants can stop at any point. Use constructed examples first. Do not request passwords, actual verification codes, financial details or unredacted messages. Do not record audio/video or collect identifying information by default.

Ask: What kinds of suspicious requests reach you? What do you normally do? Who do you ask for help? What would make a warning useful or unhelpful?

Then use four different scenarios: a code-sharing request, an ordinary code notification, a suspicious payment request, and an image with unresolved origin. Ask the participant to explain the evidence and choose a next step. Do not coach until they have answered.
